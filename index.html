Methodology, assumptions & formulas

Model. Seeded Monte Carlo draws randomly from a finite shoe of standard 52-card decks, without replacement. A new shuffled shoe is prepared between rounds after the configured penetration is reached. If a shoe runs out before the round can finish, the simulator refunds that round’s wagers, tracks the exhaustion separately and opens a fresh shoe. Each session resets player balances and starts a fresh shoe. If every seat cannot meet the minimum, that session ends early.

Rules. The standard settings retain the original game. Advanced settings configure dealer thresholds, hole-card and peek rules, double after split, maximum hands, late surrender, and natural payouts. Split aces receive one card and cannot be resplit; split 21 is not a natural. Insurance is offered by the count-deviation strategy when its index is reached. The dealer’s blackjack takes priority over surrender. All opening wagers follow the selected table, spread, bankroll, and chip constraints. With bankroll limits disabled, balances may become negative.

Strategy. The reference basic-strategy table is a multi-deck policy with soft-17 adjustments. Advanced settings add custom matrices, count deviations, and composition-dependent EV estimates, plus independent opponent play and betting. The original Hi-Lo spread is “Discrete true count.” Count display never changes counting or betting state. The information panels explain each model and distinguish exact accounting from approximate advantage and EV calculations.

Estimates. Win / loss / push shares use settled hands as the denominator. ROI = total player net profit ÷ total amount wagered, including added split and double stakes and insurance. Natural blackjack count includes pushes. The bankroll graph is for the current session; statistics pool all sessions. The histogram, median, and 5th–95th percentiles use completed sessions. After at least 30 completed sessions, the 95% session-ROI interval uses a normal approximation and a session-level ratio-estimator standard error. Let X be session profit, Y session wager, R = ΣX/ΣY, and Z = X − RY. The interval is R ± 1.96 × s(Z)/(√sessions × mean(Y)). This accounts for dependence within each session; it remains an approximation and may be unreliable for skewed outcomes or few sessions. “Any seat below minimum” counts sessions ending with at least one seat unable to make the table minimum. Outcomes within a shoe and hands sharing a dealer are dependent, so the displayed rates are estimates, not exact theoretical house-edge values. Brownian motion is not used: discrete card draws and blackjack payoffs are modeled directly.

Deck penetration

Penetration is the share of the shoe dealt before the dealer shuffles. Deeper penetration gives the remaining cards more time to differ from a fresh shoe, which can make information from the running count more useful.

All three modes place a cut card. A round can pass that card and still finish, so realized penetration can exceed the target. Let D be the deck count, N the shoe size, C the cut position, and Xᵢ the number of cards dealt in round i.

N=52D,C=⌈Npcut⌉N=52D,\quad C=\lceil Np_{\mathrm{cut}}\rceil

Fixed

Set the same cut percentage for every shoe. The default is 75%.

pcut=q100,q=entered percentagep_{\mathrm{cut}}=q/100

Simulated

The attached model samples a new cut position for each shoe. The simulator then deals actual blackjack rounds until the cut is reached. Player count, hits, splits and doubles all affect the overshoot.

pcut∼TN(0.75,0.0152;0.70,0.80)p_{\mathrm{cut}}\sim\mathrm{TN}(0.75,0.015^2;0.70,0.80)

Gaussian

Draw a cut target centered at 75% with your chosen σ in percentage points. Redraw targets outside 20–95%. A σ of zero always selects 75%. At larger σ, the asymmetric bounds pull the accepted average below 75%.

pcut∼TN(0.75,(σ/100)2;0.20,0.95)p_{\mathrm{cut}}\sim\mathrm{TN}(0.75,(\sigma/100)^2;0.20,0.95)

Realized penetration

TN denotes a truncated normal with the mean and variance of the underlying normal, followed by the acceptance bounds. The simulator measures penetration when the first round to reach the cut card finishes.

Sn=∑i=1nXi,T=min{n:Sn≥C},P=100%×STNS_n=\sum_{i=1}^n X_i,\quad T=\min\{n:S_n\ge C\},\quad P=100\%\times S_T/N

Shoes that end with a session before reaching the cut are excluded from the averages. If the shoe runs out mid-round, wagers are refunded and that shoe is counted separately. A high exhaustion count means the displayed averages cover only the shoes that could finish their final round.
